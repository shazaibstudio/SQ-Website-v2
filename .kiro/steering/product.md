# SQ Interactive — Product Overview

## What We Are

SQ Interactive is a premium digital innovation studio website showcasing four core capabilities: **Digital**, **AI**, **Immersive**, and **Lab** (R&D).

## Core Purpose

The website serves as a navigation portal and brand showcase for a creative studio based in Lahore, Pakistan. It emphasizes clarity, premium aesthetics, and problem-first thinking.

## Key Messaging

- **Hero Value Prop**: "Build what your business needs next." — Focus on business needs, not technology hype.
- **Philosophy**: "We figure out what needs fixing. Then we build the right solution — not the most impressive one."
- **CTA**: "Have a problem worth solving?" — Consultative, not pushy.

## The Four Worlds

1. **Digital** — Websites, e-commerce, software
2. **AI** — AI systems, automation, intelligent workflows
3. **Immersive** — VR, AR, 3D, interactive experiences
4. **Lab** — R&D, experiments, emerging technology

## Target Audience

- Business owners and decision-makers
- Companies seeking digital innovation
- Clients interested in AI, custom software, or immersive tech
- Primarily Pakistan-based, working internationally

## Visual Identity

- **Color**: Dark theme (#0A0A0A background) with premium gold accents (#D4AF37)
- **Typography**: Serif (Playfair Display) for headings, sans-serif (Plus Jakarta Sans) for body
- **Tone**: Confident, minimalist, no-nonsense
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, sophisticated, modern

## Key Sections

- **Hero**: Full-viewport video, headline, dual CTAs
- **Hub**: 4 navigation cards to each capability world
- **Live Proof**: 3 real client websites with live indicators
- **Philosophy**: Brand statement and values
- **CTA**: Final call-to-action section
- **Footer**: Contact info, navigation, social links

## Design Principles

1. **Navigation-First**: Clear pathways, no clutter
2. **Mobile-First**: Optimized for 320px+, scales gracefully
3. **Premium Dark Theme**: Bold, sophisticated, accessible
4. **Minimal CTA**: One floating button (right side), no nav bloat
5. **Semantic HTML**: Proper structure, accessibility first
6. **No External Dependencies**: Vanilla JS, self-hosted

## Success Metrics

- Clear navigation to four capability worlds
- Compelling hero messaging and CTAs
- Professional social proof (live client work)
- Mobile-optimized experience
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG AA)
- Fast load times, no bloat
