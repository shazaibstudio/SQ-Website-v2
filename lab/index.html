What We Do Here

Where technology meets experimentation.

SQ Lab is where we explore emerging technologies, test new approaches, and build prototypes before they become client solutions. It's how we stay ahead of trends and understand what actually works in practice — not just theory.

Everything we learn in the lab either becomes a service offering or informs how we approach client projects. It's R&D in action.
